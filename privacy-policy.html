Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information such as your email address, username, and payment details (via Razorpay) to facilitate predictions and payouts.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for account management, transaction processing, and ensuring fair play on the PROPHIT platform.

3. Third-Party Sharing

Prediction coin purchases are disabled. Configured verification and email providers process the data needed for their services. No device fingerprinting or advertiser targeting is implemented. Advertising and additional data uses require a separately reviewed consent and privacy policy before launch.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures, including bcrypt hashing for passwords and HTTPS for all network traffic, to protect your personal information.
