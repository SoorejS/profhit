Frequently Asked Questions

How are markets resolved?

Markets are resolved by administrators based on the official resolution criteria and source provided when the market was created.

What happens if a market ties?

Closest numerical answers at equal distance share the fixed reward. Other outcomes follow the published rule. Cancelled events or disputed evidence need administrator review before settlement. Prediction entry is free.

How long do rewards take to process?

Redemptions are manually reviewed to prevent fraud. Expected voucher delivery is 24–48 hours after verification and official sourcing. Check the actual status in your wallet.
