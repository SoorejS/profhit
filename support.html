Frequently Asked Questions

How are markets resolved?

Markets are resolved by administrators based on the official resolution criteria and source provided when the market was created.

What happens if a market ties?

If a market results in a tie or is cancelled, the market is resolved as "Void" and all users receive a full refund of their initial prediction.

How long do rewards take to process?

Redemptions are manually reviewed to prevent fraud. Please allow 24-48 hours for your voucher or payout to arrive.
