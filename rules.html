PROPHIT game rules

PROPHIT is a virtual coin prediction game. Each market publishes its coin entry cost and fixed correct-answer reward. Participation costs no real money and requires no purchased coins. Coins are internal rewards; users cannot transfer them or exchange them for cash.

Each earned batch expires six calendar months after earning. Spending uses the oldest valid batches first. Expired coins cannot be spent or reinstated. A reminder is issued within 30 days of expiry.

Earning coins

  • Sign-up bonus: 100 coins once on activation.
  • Daily login: 10 coins once per UTC day.
  • Complete name, email, phone, city, country and interests: 30 coins once.
  • Predict on 3 consecutive UTC days: 25 coins; 7 days: 75 coins. A milestone rewards once per uninterrupted streak.
  • Referral: 50 coins after the referred user's first valid prediction, with a 48-hour fraud-check delay. Maximum 20 bonuses.

Predictions

One prediction per category per UTC day. Each question publishes its answer format, measurable rule, approved result source and cutoff. Authorized reviewers settle from documented source evidence; AI and news do not settle rewards. Closest numerical answers that tie share the win; top-three answers require all three unique nominees, regardless of order.

Vouchers

Bronze: 500 coins → ₹50; Silver: 1,200 → ₹150; Gold: 2,500 → ₹350; Platinum: 5,000 → ₹800; Diamond: 10,000 → ₹2,000. These are voucher values, not a coin-to-cash exchange rate.

Redemption requires current KYC, phone OTP and email confirmation. KYC expires after 12 months. The contact verification integration is pending staging validation, so redemption may be unavailable.

Voucher requests show Pending, Processing, Fulfilled, Delivered or Failed. Expected delivery is 24–48 hours after verification and official sourcing; delivery depends on actual fulfilment and email dispatch. Silver–Diamond badges follow fulfilment. Gold includes leaderboard recognition. Platinum priority support and the Diamond ad slot are pending.
