# PROPHIT user guide

Register with a username, email, password, and matching password confirmation, or log in with email/password. Usernames use letters, numbers, and underscores. Email matching is case-insensitive. Enable authenticator 2FA from Settings by confirming your password, scanning the QR code and entering the current 6-digit code. Once enabled, email/password or Google Sign-In also requires the authenticator code. Disabling 2FA requires a current code. Recovery codes are not implemented, so keep access to the enrolled authenticator. Google Sign-In requires deployment configuration. Password reset uses an expiring, one-time email link and reports that the service is unavailable when SMTP is not configured.

Browse Markets and read the category, answer format, measurable rule, approved source and cutoff. Prediction entry is free, including at a zero coin balance. No stake or payment is accepted. One prediction per category per UTC day applies; submissions cannot be changed. Ranges use minimum/maximum in the published units; sports scores accept a run total or [home,away] as defined in the rule. Politics margins use a five-percentage-point tolerance; financial percentage-change guesses use ±1 percentage point. Top-three picks require all three unique nominees, in any order. Closest numerical answers tie when the distance is equal. Wild-card free text is limited to a measurable numerical answer, never subjective interpretation.

Sign-up grants 100 welcome coins once. Daily login grants 10 coins once per UTC day. Prediction days, separately tracked, grant 25 at day 3 and 75 at day 7 of an uninterrupted streak; duplicate same-day submissions do not advance it. Complete name, email, phone, city, country and interests in Settings to earn 30 coins once. KYC and 2FA do not substitute for profile fields. Other prediction-count achievements are badges with no extra coin grants.

Category payouts (Easy / Medium / Hard): Weather 20/50/120, Sports 25/60/200, Politics 30/80/250, Entertainment 25/70/150, Financial Markets 20/80/300, Wild Card 40/100/400. Weekly challenges link to a reviewed market and pay twice its normal reward to every correct participant. The challenges screen shows participation, cutoff, completion and historical results.

Wallet shows spendable virtual coins, per-batch earning/expiry dates and the ledger. Each earned batch lasts six calendar months, clamped at month ends. Redemption consumes the oldest unexpired batches. Expiry reminders are persisted once within 30 days of expiry. Expired coins cannot be spent or reinstated. Refunds restore only the original still-valid batches and never extend validity. Historical requests lacking batch allocations require operator reconciliation.

Your referral code records the relationship at signup. You receive 50 coins only after your friend’s first valid prediction and a persistent 48-hour fraud-check delay. No signup, KYC or deposit referral reward is issued. Maximum 20 bonuses. Profile shows pending/paid referral history.

Voucher tiers: Bronze 500→₹50; Silver 1,200→₹150; Gold 2,500→₹350; Platinum 5,000→₹800; Diamond 10,000→₹2,000. Redemption requires current annual KYC plus phone OTP and email confirmation. The provider contact-verification adapter is pending staging validation; document-only KYC and legacy booleans cannot redeem. KYC is valid for 12 months. Do not use the simulator as real verification.

Voucher requests move through Pending, Processing, Fulfilled, Delivered or Failed. An admin supplies an actual officially sourced voucher and supplier invoice/order reference. Email dispatch is tracked separately, with an expected 24–48-hour operational window. Delivered records SMTP acceptance, not independently confirmed inbox receipt. A failed or interrupted dispatch needs operator reconciliation before retry to prevent duplicate mail. Silver–Diamond badges are granted at fulfilment, and Gold tier appears on the existing leaderboard. Platinum priority support and the Diamond ad slot remain pending. No real voucher is generated by the application.

News requires configured provider credentials and preserves article URLs/timestamps. Fresh headlines become unpublished editorial candidates; reviewers must supply measurable rules and an approved outcome source before publication. AI/news never independently settle rewards. Live events refetch real server counters, wallet, notifications and leaderboard; reconnecting clients refetch state.

Support opens your email application; it does not create a server-side ticket. Avatar uploads and notification preferences remain unfinished. No invasive device fingerprint is collected. Monetization remains deferred until the core game and provider gates are complete.
