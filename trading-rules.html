Trading Rules

Understand how markets resolve and how payouts are calculated.

1. Market States

Markets are prepared as drafts, may be scheduled, then accept predictions while Open or Live. At the cutoff they become Locked, then Awaiting Resolution or Resolved. Resolved markets may be archived.

2. Free Predictions

Participation uses the published virtual coin entry cost. Coins are earned in the game; cash payments and coin purchases are disabled. You may make one prediction per market and one prediction per category per UTC calendar day. Predictions cannot be edited or cancelled. Read the published answer format, measurable outcome rule, source and deadline.

3. Payout Calculation

Each category and difficulty has a fixed coin reward, shown before confirmation. Correct weekly challenge predictions earn twice the normal payout. Incorrect predictions receive no payout. Earned coins expire after six calendar months. See all reward, format and redemption rules.

4. Dispute Resolution

If you believe a resolution is incorrect, contact Support with the market ID and evidence. Payouts are recorded when the admin resolves the market. The application does not implement a dispute holding period or automatically clear wallet history.
