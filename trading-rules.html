Trading Rules

Understand how markets resolve and how payouts are calculated.

1. Market States

Markets move through three states: Active (Open for predictions), Locked (Waiting for outcome), and Resolved (Outcome finalized and payouts distributed).

2. Prediction Stakes

Once a prediction is submitted, the staked amount is deducted from your wallet and cannot be refunded unless the market is cancelled by an administrator.

3. Payout Calculation

Payouts are calculated based on the fixed odds at the exact time your prediction was placed. The potential return is displayed before you confirm your trade.

4. Dispute Resolution

If you believe a market was resolved incorrectly, you have 24 hours to file a dispute via the Support page before payouts are finalized and finalized ledgers are cleared.
